ℹ️ Google IMA vs Unity Ads

  • Google IMA: Real web video ads with actual revenue
  • Unity Ads: Mobile-only SDK, doesn't work in browsers
  • VAST Protocol: Industry standard for video advertising
  • Ad Networks: Works with Google Ad Manager, AdSense, and others
  • Revenue: Generates actual income from completed video views
  • Fill Rate: Higher ad availability than mobile-focused networks
