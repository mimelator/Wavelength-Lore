DEATH SCENE


A photorealistic scene of a pig-snouted, grotesquely fat goblin king on a portable throne with his eyes closed, looking peaceful with some drool coming out of his mouth.  
In front of the goblin king's mouth is a classic Stage microphone.  

The Stage Microphone has an shining arrow that has fully bisected the microphone and is slightly sticking into the adam's apple of the Goblin King and just barely touching the adam's apple of the goblin grotesquely fat king's throat.

The skies are dark and lightning can be seen in the distance.
